# Adjudication fixtures

`AdjudicationSetFactory.build200AnswerSet()` creates a deterministic test set:

- 10 rubric slices.
- 20 answer variants per slice.
- 200 total adjudication cases.
- 2 generated reviewer labels per case.

Answers, expected bands, and reviewer labels are generated by the factory. They test the evaluation pipeline's shape and repeatability; they are not human annotations or evidence of agreement between human reviewers and a model.
